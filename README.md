# Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard – Excel Project

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing hospital Emergency Room (ER) data using an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard. It provides insights into patient attendance, admission status, patient satisfaction, average waiting time, department referrals, gender distribution, and monthly patient activity.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze emergency-room patient attendance.
- Compare admitted and non-admitted patients.
- Monitor patient satisfaction and average waiting time.
- Identify departments receiving the highest number of referrals.
- Understand gender-wise patient distribution.
- Analyze monthly patient activity and variations in patient volume.

## 📊 Key Insights

### 1. Patient Attendance Status
Patient attendance status helps identify potential waiting-time concerns and areas where hospital management may need to investigate delays.

### 2. Admission Status
The numbers of admitted and non-admitted patients are fairly balanced, providing an overview of patient outcomes following emergency-room visits.

### 3. Patient Satisfaction
Patient satisfaction can be monitored alongside waiting time to understand whether delays may be affecting the patient experience.

### 4. Average Waiting Time
Average waiting time is an important KPI for monitoring emergency-room service levels and evaluating changes in operational efficiency over time.

### 5. Department Referrals
Orthopedics appears to have the highest number of referrals among the departments shown. This insight can help management understand specialty demand and consider resource allocation.

### 6. Gender Distribution
The dashboard displays the gender-wise distribution of emergency-room visits during the selected period.

### 7. Monthly Patient Activity
The monthly activity chart helps identify variations in patient visits across months. Month and year slicers allow users to compare periods and identify times of higher patient volume.

## 🛠️ Tools and Features
- Microsoft Excel
- Interactive dashboard and charts
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Month and year slicers
- Data filtering and comparative analysis
- Patient activity and operational performance monitoring

## 💡 Business Value
The dashboard brings key emergency-room indicators together in one place. It helps hospital management explore patient activity, monitor waiting-time performance, understand referral demand, and identify monthly patient-volume trends.

These insights can support data-informed decisions and highlight areas for further investigation.

## 📝 Conclusion
The Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard provides an interactive overview of emergency-room performance and patient activity. Its visualizations and filtering options enable users to explore trends, compare key indicators, and support operational decision-making.
